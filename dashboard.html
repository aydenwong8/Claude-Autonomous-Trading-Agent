Holdings

Day change is per-position intraday P&L from the broker. Its total can differ slightly from the account-level Day P&L tile, which is measured against yesterday's closing equity and also reflects cash and any positions opened or closed today.

Cumulative return, indexed to 100 at inception

Agent equity and SPY closes are paired by trading-day sequence, not raw date string — Alpaca's account-history day markers and market-data bar dates don't always land on identical UTC calendar days.

Behavioural metrics

These measure how the agent behaves, not how it performs — turnover, how often its own guardrails reject it, and whether its verdicts are stable. Rates that need more history than exists are shown as — rather than a number, since a ratio over a handful of decisions is noise. Reaction to volatility and news events is not measured here; it needs per-symbol price and news history joined to decision timestamps.
